1. Who we are

Owen Le, trading as DueForm (“DueForm”, “we”, “us”), is based in London, United Kingdom. Owen Le is responsible for data protection. For any question about this policy or your personal data, email taivan@hotmail.co.uk.

We help businesses check that their invoices match their customer contracts. This policy explains how we handle personal data when we contact businesses, work with clients, and run our website.

3. How we use it, and our lawful basis

4. Data in our clients’ documents

When a client asks us to check its invoices against its customer contracts, those documents may contain personal data, such as names and contact details of people at the client’s customers. For that data we act as a processor on the client’s instructions, under a written data processing agreement. The client is the controller and its own privacy notice applies. We use that data only to provide the service, never for our own purposes or to train AI models, and we delete it at the end of the engagement (normally within 30 days of delivery).

If the client agrees, we use an AI provider to help read contracts (section 5). Otherwise we enter contract terms by hand.

6. International transfers

Some of the providers above process data outside the UK, mainly in the USA. Where they do, the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US “data bridge” for certified US companies), or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses, or another safeguard allowed by the UK GDPR. Email us for details of the safeguard for a particular provider.

8. Your rights

You have the right to: access your personal data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict how we use it; object to our use of it; and receive it in a portable format. You can object to marketing at any time, and we will stop. Just reply “stop” to any message or email us.

We will respond within one month. Some rights are limited (for example, we must keep accounting records for the period the law requires). For data in a client’s documents (section 4), contact that client, and we will help them respond.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please complain to us first at taivan@hotmail.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond fully without undue delay.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

10. Our website

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It uses your browser’s temporary session storage only to remember display state while you move between pages, such as page transitions. This is cleared when you close the tab and is not used to identify or track you. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

If you choose to book a call, the booking calendar loads from Calendly. Calendly, and services it uses such as Stripe, then set their own cookies. Nothing from Calendly loads until you open the calendar.

Our hosting provider (GitHub) keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, to run and secure the service.

We will update this policy when the way we handle personal data changes, and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.
