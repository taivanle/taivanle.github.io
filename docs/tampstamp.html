FULL-STACK PRODUCT · CAFÉ LOYALTY

A loyalty loop for independent cafés.

A digital stamp and coffee-gifting platform with rotating QR codes, database-enforced access rules, and a vendor portal.

The challenge

Replacing paper loyalty cards needs a fast customer experience and controls that vendors can rely on. Stamps must be tied to the right café and user, and a QR scan must not create repeated rewards.

System approach

01Vendor QR session
02Customer scan
03Database validation
04Stamp or reward

Conceptual flow · simplified to explain the engineering approach.

THE DESIGN DECISION

Enforce the loyalty rules in database functions and constraints. The customer interface initiates a scan; the database validates the token, ownership, expiry, and duplicate-stamp condition.

What I built

  1. Built customer and vendor journeys with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Supabase authentication, Postgres, and row-level security.
  2. Implemented short-lived rotating QR sessions and database functions for stamp issuance and reward redemption. A uniqueness constraint limits each customer card to one stamp per QR rotation.
  3. Added vendor onboarding, guarded café management, loyalty programme editing, and a portal displaying customers, stamps, and redemptions.
  4. Built a Stripe Checkout coffee-gifting flow with addressed and shareable gifts, vendor redemption, and reimbursement tracking.

Scope & reflection

A private product build. The repository documents implemented loyalty, vendor, and gifting flows; this portfolio does not claim a public launch, adoption, or revenue. Paid loyalty upgrades remain separate from the implemented gifting checkout, and app-store release requires further work.

What I take forward

The simplest customer interaction can have a substantial system behind it. Put the invariant where every client has to obey it.
