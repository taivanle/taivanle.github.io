The challenge

Negotiated terms, amendments, discounts, and renewal increases can drift from what is billed. A useful finance tool must show the source of a finding and its calculation, and distinguish a potential difference from a confirmed error or recovered payment.

THE DESIGN DECISION

Use the model to propose structured terms, then make approval and arithmetic explicit. The billing checker runs against approved data, making a finding traceable to both contract evidence and a reproducible calculation.

What I built

  1. Built a local Node.js application with owner and client logins, separate ledgers, invitations, membership revocation, and workspace-specific reports.
  2. Added document and invoice imports, a replaceable AI extraction provider, and manual term entry. Reviewers see exact supporting excerpts beside proposed terms and approve them before checking invoices.
  3. Implemented deterministic comparison of approved terms and before-tax invoice amounts, including multiple charges, supported seat-based pricing, and monthly, quarterly, and annual periods.
  4. Built findings review, renewal tracking, billing setup sheets, and PDF, CSV, and HTML evidence exports. Potential differences, confirmed errors, corrections, and collected cash retain distinct statuses.

Scope & reflection

A private local MVP with synthetic verification and a separate evaluation dataset. Customer demand and commercial outcomes remain unvalidated. Live extraction accuracy has not yet been established by the planned independent baseline. Billacord is the current working name; older folders use ClauseTally or ReplyLedger.

What I take forward

Finance reviewers need evidence they can inspect. Treating extraction, approval, calculation, and resolution as separate stages makes the product easier to audit and improve.
