02 / THE PROBLEM

The agreement and the invoice should tell the same story.

Negotiated terms, amendments, discounts, and renewal increases can drift from what is billed. A useful finance tool must show the source of a finding and its calculation, and distinguish a potential difference from a confirmed error or recovered payment.

01Negotiated amendments
02Renewal price changes
03Evidence for finance review

The problem that shaped the engineering decisions.

03 / THE ARCHITECTURE

Follow the system.

Select a stage to explore the design.

STAGE 01

Contract & amendments

Import the contract and supplied amendments. Preserve the original sources so proposed terms can be traced to the agreement.

STAGE 02

Evidence-backed proposal

Propose structured commercial terms with exact supporting excerpts, using a replaceable AI provider or manual entry.

STAGE 03

Human approval

Let the reviewer inspect the evidence, resolve ambiguities, and explicitly approve the terms that the checker will use.

STAGE 04

Invoice check & report

Compare supported billing amounts deterministically and export the calculation and evidence. A potential difference still requires finance review.

KEY DESIGN DECISION

Use the model to propose structured terms, then make approval and arithmetic explicit. The billing checker runs against approved data, making a finding traceable to both contract evidence and a reproducible calculation.

04 / THE BUILD

What I designed and implemented.

01

Client workspaces

Built a local Node.js application with owner and client logins, separate ledgers, invitations, membership revocation, and workspace-specific reports.

02

Extraction & approval

Added document and invoice imports, a replaceable AI extraction provider, and manual term entry. Reviewers see exact supporting excerpts beside proposed terms and approve them before checking invoices.

03

Billing rules

Implemented deterministic comparison of approved terms and before-tax invoice amounts, including multiple charges, supported seat-based pricing, and monthly, quarterly, and annual periods.

04

Review & reporting

Built findings review, renewal tracking, billing setup sheets, and PDF, CSV, and HTML evidence exports. Potential differences, confirmed errors, corrections, and collected cash retain distinct statuses.

06 / THE PERSPECTIVE

What I take forward.

Finance reviewers need evidence they can inspect. Treating extraction, approval, calculation, and resolution as separate stages makes the product easier to audit and improve.
SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

A private local MVP with synthetic verification and a separate evaluation dataset. Customer demand and commercial outcomes remain unvalidated. Live extraction accuracy has not yet been established by the planned independent baseline.

Key design decision

AI proposes structured terms. A person reviews and approves. Deterministic code calculates and compares billing amounts.

Evidence boundary

Validate provider output against the schema and source excerpts. A valid quote alone does not prove correct interpretation.

Billing rules

Money uses integer minor units. Supported recurring periods, multiple charges and seat pricing. Ambiguous periods require review.

Deployment scope

Local browser and Node.js server. JSON files; no external database. Separate ledgers per client. Local MVP / scoped evaluation.
